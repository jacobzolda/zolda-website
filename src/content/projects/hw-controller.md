Modified a standard PS5 controller with 4 back buttons using an ExtremeRate kit and a
soldering station. Roughly half the cost of buying a manufacturer pro controller
with fewer back buttons.


<h2 class="font-bold underline">Cost Comparison:</h2>

- Manufacturer pro controller (2 back buttons): **$200**
- This mod (4 back buttons): $70 controller + $52 ExtremeRate kit = **$122**
