Follow along the development journey of Vox Ex Machina! The initial prototype was
built during an interdisciplinary undergraduate class. The performance version was
completed as an independent study via a collaboration between the ExCITe Center at
Drexel University and Opera Philadelphia. The machine was featured at the Academy of
Music for the 50th Anniversary Gala.


<h2 class="font-bold underline">What I Built:</h2>

As lead electronics engineer and circuit designer, I designed a 3-axis
motion-control system using NEMA 17 stepper motors driven by TMC2209 drivers connected to an
Arduino Mega for synchronized multi-belt movement. The machine was integrated with
Max/MSP 9 for real-time, audio-driven control, translating live vocals into the
machine's motion and visual output on stage.

<h2 class="font-bold underline">Process:</h2>

The system went through multiple iterations: 3D modeling, breadboard circuit
prototyping, and finally a permanent protoboard build rated for live performance
conditions.

<h2 class="font-bold underline">Outcome:</h2>

The finished machine performed on the main stage of the Academy of Music for Opera
Philadelphia's 50th Anniversary Gala.
